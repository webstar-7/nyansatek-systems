About us

Built in Obuasi, for businesses like yours.

NYANSATEK Systems is a Ghanaian software business based in Obuasi, Ashanti Region. We build practical, everyday business software — a point-of-sale system for shops, a school management system for schools and similar institutions, and a gold-buying system for gold dealerships — and sell it directly to the businesses that use it, with no reseller or agency in between.

The name comes from nyansa, the Twi word for wisdom. The idea behind it is simple: good software should make a business run a little smarter, not add more work on top of what owners already have to manage by hand.

What we do

We currently offer three products:

  • NYANSATEK POS — a point-of-sale system for shops and small retail businesses, covering sales, stock across warehouse and store, credit invoices, cashier accounts, and daily reporting.
  • NYANSATEK School — an attendance, grading, and parent-communication system built for schools and similar institutions.
  • NYANSATEK Gold — a gold-buying system for dealerships: Refined and Box purchases priced automatically, a built-in karat density chart, daily cash float and balance reconciliation, and stock on hand.

All three share one support line, and are designed to be paid for and set up the same day — by Mobile Money or card, with no sales call or installation visit required.

How we work

We're a small, founder-led team. Robert Ofori-Boahene leads the business and is directly reachable through the contact details below — when you call or email NYANSATEK, you're reaching the people who actually build and maintain the software, not a call centre reading from a script.

We're still early. Our products are live and in active use, and we're building out our customer base deliberately, one business at a time, rather than growing for its own sake.

Frequently asked questions

Questions people actually ask us.

If your question isn't answered below, call 0536 340 578 or email support@nyansatek.systems.

How quickly can I start using a system after I pay?
Usually within a minute of payment clearing. Once Paystack confirms your Mobile Money or card payment, our system automatically creates your account, sets up starter data, and sends your login by SMS and email — no one has to manually set anything up.
What payment methods do you accept?
Mobile Money and card payments, processed securely through Paystack. All plans are priced in Ghana cedis (GH₵) — there's no dollar conversion or foreign exchange charge.
Do I need any special equipment or a strong internet connection?
No. NYANSATEK POS, School, and Gold all run in a web browser on a phone, tablet, or computer, and are built to stay usable on ordinary mobile data — the kind most shops and schools already have.
Can I add staff or cashier accounts?
Yes. NYANSATEK POS lets the business owner create cashier logins with their own username and password, separate from the owner's own login, so staff can use the till without having access to reports or settings.
What happens to my data if I stop paying?
Your account and data are retained for a reasonable period after a missed payment in case you want to reactivate. If you'd like your data removed sooner, contact us at support@nyansatek.systems and we'll handle it directly.
Is my business data shared with other customers?
No. Each business's data is isolated in its own secured space. Nothing is shared or visible between different NYANSATEK customers.
Can I switch plans later, or use more than one product?
Yes. You can change plans or add another NYANSATEK product from your account without re-entering your business details from scratch. Contact support and we'll help you make the change.
What if something goes wrong with my account or a payment?
Call or email us — see the details above. Because we built these systems ourselves, we can usually diagnose and fix an issue directly, rather than passing you between departments.
