NYANSATEK Restaurant

The till, the kitchen screen and the ledger, in one place. Built for restaurants, chop bars, eateries and anyone who sells cooked food: take the order, send it to the kitchen, and see the day's sales.

What's included

  • Orders for eat in, takeaway and delivery, with table numbers
  • Kitchen screen: every order as a ticket, from new to served
  • Menu by section, with a sold-out switch for each dish

  • Cash, Mobile Money, and card payments

  • Cashier accounts with their own logins
  • Profit, expense, and income-statement reports
