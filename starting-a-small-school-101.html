Please read: what it is and isn't

This is general education written to be useful anywhere in the world. It is not legal, accounting, tax or education-authority advice, and makes no promise of profit, enrolment or results. Requirements for registering and running a school differ by country, region and school type; confirm them with your education authority and a qualified adviser before you enrol children. All numbers in the examples are illustrations.
