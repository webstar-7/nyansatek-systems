About us

NYANSATEK is a Ghanaian business based in Obuasi, Ashanti Region. We build and run our own software products. NYANSATEK POS, School and Gold are all live, in active use, and not going anywhere.

We also take on a client's IT entirely, from websites to hardware, software and infrastructure, as an outsourced partner, the same way we've built and supported our own systems.

The name comes from nyansa, the Twi word for wisdom: good IT work is about judgment, not just delivery. NYANSATEK is founder-led. Robert Ofori-Boahene leads the business and is directly reachable on 0536 340 578.
