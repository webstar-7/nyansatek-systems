About us

The same NYANSATEK, a wider focus.

NYANSATEK is a Ghanaian business based in Obuasi, Ashanti Region. We build and run our own software products — NYANSATEK POS, School, and Gold are all live, in active use, and not going anywhere.

This page is about the other half of what we do: taking on a client's IT entirely — hardware, software, infrastructure — as an outsourced partner, the same way we've built and supported our own systems. Refocusing doesn't mean stepping away from the software; it means offering the same judgment and hands-on support to businesses, enterprises, and government bodies who need it.

The name comes from nyansa, the Twi word for wisdom — the idea that good IT work is about judgment, not just delivery.
