Employee Management

NYANSATEK HRMS

The NYANSATEK Employee Management System. Every employee file in order, leave approved in a tap, attendance recorded at the gate, and payroll that ends with a payslip for each person, all from a phone, a tablet or the office computer.

What's included

  • Employee files with personal details, job, contracts and documents
  • Alerts before contracts and documents expire
  • Departments and positions
  • Leave requests, approvals and balances
  • Clock-in and clock-out, and who is in today
  • Gate scanning and printed ID cards
  • Payroll runs, payslips, salary advances and staff loans
  • Recruitment, performance reviews and training records
  • Announcements, company documents, video calls and meetings
  • A login for every employee, and an activity log of who changed what

Specifications

DevicesAny browser — phone, tablet, or desktop. Can be installed like an app.
AccessOwner, admin, HR officer, payroll and employee roles
PrivacyPersonal details and salaries stay with HR and payroll; managers see only their team
Staff sign-inBy email, or by organization code and staff ID
